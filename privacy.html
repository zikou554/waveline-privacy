This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it. 1. Information We Collect Waveline does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. The App does not require you to: Create an account Sign in Provide your name or email address Submit any personal information 2. Permissions Used The App requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality: Internet Access – To stream radio stations and podcasts. Network State – To detect network connectivity and improve playback. Foreground Service – To allow audio playback while the App is running in the background or when the screen is off. Bluetooth – To support playback through Bluetooth audio devices. These permissions are used solely to operate the App and are not used for tracking, profiling, or collecting personal information. 3. Third-Party Content The radio stations and podcasts available in Waveline are provided through third-party streaming services. When connecting to these services, they may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address as part of the normal internet communication required to deliver audio streams. Waveline does not control how these third-party providers process such information. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the respective content providers if you have questions about their data practices. 4. Children's Privacy Waveline does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal data, it is suitable for users of all ages. 5.
